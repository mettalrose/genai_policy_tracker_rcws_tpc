About this tracker

The larger project

This tracker is part of a longer project analyzing emerging trends in how our fields are responding to generative AI, particularly in terms of changing scholarly norms. We will release our analysis of these journals in the coming months.

What's included, and what's missing

The tracker lists only journals for which we located a generative AI policy. If a journal from our fields is not listed, that is only because we could not find such a policy on the journal's website. If you know of a journal with a policy we have missed, or you see a broken or incorrect link, please email Anuj Gupta at anujguptaresearch7@gmail.com.

How the data was collected

The data was collected by Anuj Gupta and Susan Miller-Cochran over several months, taking inspiration from a similar data collection project that Chris Anson began a few years ago. Policy links were last checked in January 2026. The website was designed by Anuj Gupta with assistance from Claude.

Using this data
