About this tracker

The larger project

This tracker is part of a longer project analyzing emerging trends in how our fields are responding to generative AI, particularly in terms of changing scholarly norms. We will release our analysis of these journals in the coming months.

What's included, and what's missing

The tracker lists only journals for which we located a generative AI policy. If a journal from our fields is not listed, that is only because we could not find such a policy on the journal's website. If you know of a journal with a policy we have missed, or you see a broken or incorrect link, please use our reporting form. For anything else, you can email Anuj Gupta at anujguptaresearch7 [at] gmail [dot] com.

How the data was collected

Building the journal list. We started with the list of journals in rhetoric, composition and writing studies, and technical and professional communication published by Doug Hesse (2019), and added to it based on our knowledge of journals in the field. The project also took inspiration from Chris Anson's presentation at the Eleventh International Writing Analytics Conference (2025). No list of journals in a field can probably ever be exhaustive, since what counts as a field and what counts as a journal within it are subjective to a degree. This is why we welcome your input if you'd like to add suggest other additions to our list.

First round (January 2026). Anuj Gupta and Susan Miller-Cochran checked each journal's website for a generative AI policy, using search engines like Google and DuckDuckGo to locate journal, publisher and organizational policies.

Update (October 2026). We used the web search and browsing tools of Claude Opus 5.5, a Gen AI model made by Anthropic, to check whether any journals had published a policy since January, re-checking each journal for which we had not found one. Following Anthropic's guidelines, Claude respected each website's robots.txt rules, and we additionally instructed it to keep its requests to a minimum so as not to burden any website's servers. We manually verified every result Claude found by checking the policy ourselves.

The website was designed by Anuj Gupta with assistance from Claude.
